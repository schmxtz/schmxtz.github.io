Read aloud

Paste a link to a Wikipedia article and listen to it, read in my voice. Only the running text is read: no captions, tables or references.

How it works

The voice is a Piper model fine-tuned on half an hour of my recordings. It runs entirely in your browser, so the first visit downloads about 80 MB. After that it is stored and starts quickly.

The page reads one sentence while it prepares the next few. It keeps playing with the screen locked, and you can pause or skip paragraphs from the lock screen or your headphones. On a computer: space pauses, the arrow keys skip paragraphs.

Names and foreign words are sometimes mispronounced, since the pronunciation comes from rules rather than from my recordings.
